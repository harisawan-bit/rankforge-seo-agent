# SEO Brief Example — "Emergency Plumber in Austin"

> Illustrative example output produced by the RankForge SEO workflow.
> Not a substitute for a live audit.

## Target
- Page: `https://example-plumbing.com/austin-emergency-plumber`
- Goal: Rank in the local pack and organic results for "emergency plumber Austin"
- Conversion: phone calls / booked jobs

## Primary Keywords
- Emergency plumber Austin (high intent, high competition)
- 24/7 plumber Austin TX
- Burst pipe repair Austin

## Content Recommendations
- Lead with a clear H1, service area, and a tap-to-call button above the fold.
- Add an FAQ block: pricing, response time, service areas, warranties.
- Include localized proof: "Licensed in Travis County", real neighborhood names.
- Add schema: `LocalBusiness` + `Service` + `FAQPage` + `AggregateRating` if eligible.

## Technical Quick Wins
- Compress the hero image and enable lazy loading on below-fold media.
- Keep a single canonical unless there is a real multi-region need.
- Ensure mobile tap targets are >= 48px and the NAP (name/address/phone) is consistent sitewide.

## Measurement
- Track calls from the landing page, Google Business Profile views, and "direction" requests.
- Review Search Console impressions/CTR for the target queries weekly.

## Personalized Human-Only Off-Page SEO Recommendations
- Claim and fully complete the Google Business Profile; post weekly updates.
- Ask recent customers for Google reviews that name the service and the city.
- Sponsor a local little-league team or neighborhood event for a citation + link.
- Join the Austin apartment managers' association for referral partnerships.
